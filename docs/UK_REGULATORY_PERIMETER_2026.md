# XQP — UK Regulatory Perimeter Map

**Snapshot:** 5 September 2026
**Purpose:** Engineering/compliance planning, not legal advice.

## Current design rule

No XQP feature is to be treated as legally cleared merely because it is technically possible.

## Current UK position

The existing UK cryptoasset financial-promotions regime applies to qualifying cryptoasset promotions capable of having an effect in the UK. Public websites and social-media communications can fall within scope. XQP public-facing material therefore requires a promotion-compliance review before it is used to induce UK consumers to acquire XQP.

The FCA's new comprehensive cryptoasset regime is scheduled to commence on 25 October 2027. The application period is scheduled for 30 September 2026 to 28 February 2027. Existing registrations do not automatically become authorisation under the new regime.

Potential future regulated activities relevant to the XQP roadmap include:

- issuing a qualifying stablecoin;
- safeguarding cryptoassets;
- operating a cryptoasset trading platform;
- dealing or arranging deals in qualifying cryptoassets;
- staking and certain intermediary activities.

The exact perimeter depends on the implemented business model, not the project's terminology.

## XQP feature gates

### Green — research/development
Generally suitable to progress now, subject to ordinary law:
- open protocol specifications;
- testnet software;
- non-custodial prototypes;
- security research;
- accounting and transparency dashboards using public data;
- asset registry and developer tooling.

### Amber — compliance review before launch
- merchant crypto settlement;
- exchange/quote routing;
- custody integrations;
- staking functionality;
- consumer acquisition journeys;
- OTC dealing/arranging;
- financial promotions;
- token incentives.

### Red — no production launch without specific legal/regulatory clearance
- XQP-SV issuance;
- holding customer money or cryptoassets as custodian where regulated;
- operating a regulated trading venue;
- representing XQP as legal tender, a bank deposit, guaranteed investment or regulated stablecoin when it is not;
- promising guaranteed returns or capital protection without a lawful factual basis.

## Immediate compliance workstream

1. Determine the legal entity intended to develop/operate XQP.
2. Map every proposed service to the Cryptoassets Regulations and FCA perimeter guidance.
3. Audit all existing website, buy, OTC and social copy for UK financial-promotion risk.
4. Determine which lawful financial-promotion route, if any, applies before further UK consumer marketing.
5. Decide whether the intended 2027 business model requires an FCA application during the scheduled gateway window.
6. Obtain specialist UK crypto regulatory advice before launching regulated functionality or XQP-SV.

## Engineering implication

Compliance is a protocol capability: transaction policy, jurisdiction flags, audit trails and modular identity controls should exist before they are urgently required. However, XQP must not collect identity data merely for appearance's sake; collection must have a defined lawful and operational purpose.
