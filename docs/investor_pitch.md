# XQP – Investor / Grant / Award Pitch (Draft v1)

**Why it exists**
- Most tokens talk about “doing good” later; XQP bakes it in from day 1.
- The founder has a lived story (legal hardship, homelessness, Manchester base) that makes this far more than a memecoin.
- The UK / church / charity angle is under‑served in Web3 – this is a chance to show the sector how to do transparent giving.

4. **Media & content** – to push the story out of Manchester: "faith + Web3 + social good".

**Why back this**
